Scoreza Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

1. About Scoreza

Scoreza is a padel scorekeeping application for Android phones and compatible Wear OS watches.

Developer: Scoreza
Privacy contact: aoliverayperez@gmail.com

2. Information stored on your devices

Scoreza allows you to manage padel match scores, player names, match settings and match history. This information is stored locally on your device where the relevant feature is used. Scoreza does not operate its own user-account system or backend server to collect match information.

3. Phone and watch communication

When you use the phone and watch apps together, they exchange information needed to keep the scoring experience synchronised using Google's Wear OS Data Layer services. The information exchanged may include match and scoring information. Communication depends on the supported connection between your paired devices and Wear OS services.

Scoreza does not use this feature to send match information to the developer.

4. Voice announcements and notifications

Scoreza can announce scores using your Android device's text-to-speech functionality. Speech processing depends on the text-to-speech engine installed and selected on your device.

Scoreza may also show a notification while a match is in progress and score announcements are active.

5. Data collection and sharing

Scoreza does not have an account system, advertising or analytics features. The developer does not operate a server that collects match scores or match history.

Scoreza does not intentionally transmit your match information to the developer or sell it to third parties. Google Play services, Wear OS services and your selected text-to-speech engine may process technical information as part of their own operation and are subject to their respective privacy policies.

6. Internet access

Scoreza does not require an account or a Scoreza-operated online service to keep match scores. Phone-to-watch communication depends on the connectivity and services supported by your devices.

7. Data retention and deletion

Match information is retained on your device until you delete it through available app functionality or remove the app, subject to the Android and Wear OS storage behaviour of your device.

If you uninstall Scoreza, locally stored app data is generally removed by the operating system. Any data retained separately by device services is subject to the relevant service's policies.

8. Children's privacy

Scoreza does not ask users to provide personal information or create accounts. The app's handling of data is described in this policy regardless of the user's age.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if Scoreza's features or data practices change. The latest version will be available at the public privacy-policy URL provided in the Google Play listing.
